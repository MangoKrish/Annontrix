001 — THE LEDE01 / 05

Built properly, on purpose.

Annontrix is a small, senior software studio. We build the whole thing — the data model, the backend, the interface, the deploy pipeline — for businesses that are tired of tools that almost fit. Most of our work starts with a real bottleneck: a back office run on spreadsheets, a website that loads slowly and converts worse, an app idea that has been "almost ready" for a year.

A thread runs through how we work: software should respect the people paying for it and the people using it. That's why our projects turn out fixed-scope, demoed weekly, documented plainly, and handed over with full source ownership — no lock-in, no surprise invoices, no account managers between you and the engineers.

Right now we're taking on software solutions, web development, and app development projects — and we come back to every brief within one business day.
